The Stripes Series

Small browser games that each make one climate idea physical. Every number comes from a published dataset through a reproducible pipeline, and every simplification is stated on screen.

Episode 1

Height Check

Measure your height for a week and learn nothing. Then walk 176 years of global temperature, one step per year, and see the staircase become the warming stripes.

A trend is a property of the long view, not of any one day or year.

 Episode 2

Carbon Road

Drive from 1850 to 2024 through four energy eras. Then go electric, and watch the bar stop climbing without ever coming down.

Prototype: keyboard and tap only, no text version yet, and its numbers are not yet through the data pipeline.

Warming tracks the total ever emitted. Stopping is not undoing.

 Episode 3

Loaded Dice

Catch thirty summers of real daily temperatures, then thirty more. The middle of the pile barely moves. The hot edge nearly doubles.

A small shift in the average makes extremes much more common.
Built with a shared pixel engine. Data: HadCRUT5 (Met Office Hadley Centre / CRU), Global Carbon Budget via Our World in Data, Tierney et al. 2020. Warming stripes concept by Ed Hawkins, showyourstripes.info (CC BY 4.0). Source on GitHub.
