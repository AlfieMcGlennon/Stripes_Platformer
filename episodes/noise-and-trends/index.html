Noise and Trends is a short interactive walk about the difference between day-to-day weather noise and a long-term climate trend. This browser cannot draw it. The argument is this: any single day, month or year tells you almost nothing, because the wobble between them is larger than the trend underneath. Average enough of them together and the wobble cancels while the trend does not. The global temperature record since 1850 rises by about 1.4 degrees Celsius against its 1850-1900 average, and the ten warmest years in it are all since 2015.
