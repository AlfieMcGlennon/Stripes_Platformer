Forecaster is a short interactive about how far ahead weather can be predicted. A forecast here is a weighted sum of today's temperature anomaly at four places: central England, Valentia in Ireland, De Bilt in the Netherlands and Balmoral in Scotland. You choose the weights and the equation is fitted against every day from 1960 to 2025. Copying today's weather works tomorrow and is worse than useless three days out. Even the best possible weights lose useful skill after about eight days, because there is no information left in those thermometers about that day. Then the same record is asked a different question: not what next Tuesday will be, but whether the next thirty years will average warmer than the last thirty. Asked of a single year that is a coin flip, right about half the time. Asked of thirty years since 1970 it has been right every single time. Nobody can tell you the temperature three weeks from Tuesday; that is not the same as nobody knowing which way the next thirty years go.
