Carbon Road is a short interactive about carbon dioxide accumulating in the atmosphere. You drive from 1850 to 2024 through four energy eras — horse and cart, steam, the motor car, the jet — while a bar along the top shows the total ever emitted. When you switch to electric at the end, the exhaust stops but the bar only stops climbing; it never comes back down. That is what net zero does: it stops the warming getting worse, and does not undo it.
