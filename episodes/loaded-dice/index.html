Loaded Dice is a short interactive about extreme heat. Summer days from the central England daily temperature record fall down a temperature axis, so the pile they land in draws the distribution. You play thirty summers from 1961 to 1990, then thirty from 1996 to 2025. The average summer day is about one degree warmer in the second period — roughly a third of the spread that already existed — but days reaching 28 °C or more happen about 1.8 times as often, and the further out you look the bigger the multiple becomes. A small shift in the middle of a distribution is a large change at its edge.
